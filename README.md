## About SlickClip

SlickClip is a lightweight Windows replay application built for capturing the moments you actually want to keep.

Start the Replay Buffer, play normally, and press your configured hotkey whenever something worth saving happens.

SlickClip saves the **previous** 30 seconds, 1 minute, 2 minutes, 3 minutes, or 5 minutes without requiring you to record entire gaming sessions.

Everything stays on your PC.

---

## Features

### Instant Replay
- Configurable 30 sec / 1 / 2 / 3 / 5 minute replay buffer
- Custom global Save Replay hotkey
- Display, window, and game capture
- 30 FPS and 60 FPS capture
- 720p, 1080p, and 1440p workflows
- Automatic encoder selection

### Separate Audio
Keep audio sources independently adjustable:

- Game Audio
- Voice Chat / Discord
- Microphone
- Other application audio

### Clip Library
- Automatic thumbnails
- Search and sorting
- Favorites
- Collections
- Protect from Cleanup
- Multi-select and batch actions
- Recently watched information
- Play counts
- Adjustable grid sizes

### Built-in Editor
Quickly make a clip ready to share without opening another editing application.

- Trim
- Split
- Delete sections
- Rejoin edits
- Undo / Redo
- Independent audio mixing
- Volume adjustment up to 300%
- Export to a new video

### Replay Roulette
Can't decide what clip to watch?

Let SlickClip randomly choose one from your library.

### Automatic Game Detection
SlickClip can recognize active games and automatically prepare Replay for approved titles.

### Local Storage Management
Set a storage limit and SlickClip can automatically remove the oldest clips when space is needed.

Clips marked **Protected from Cleanup** are excluded.

## Getting Started

1. Install SlickClip.
2. Choose your game, window, or display.
3. Start **Replay Buffer**.
4. Play normally.
5. Press your **Save Replay** hotkey when something happens.
6. Open **Clips** to watch, organize, edit, or share it.

SlickClip keeps only the most recent configured replay window available until you choose to save it.

## Windows SmartScreen

SlickClip is currently distributed without a commercial Windows Authenticode certificate.

Because of this, Windows may initially display an **Unknown Publisher** or Microsoft Defender SmartScreen warning.

If SmartScreen appears:

1. Select **More info**
2. Select **Run anyway**

SlickClip's built-in update packages are separately cryptographically signed and verified before installation.

---

## Updates

SlickClip includes a built-in updater.

Once installed:

**Settings → Advanced → Updates → Check for Updates**

Future releases can be downloaded and installed directly from SlickClip.
